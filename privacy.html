MD-Reviewer Privacy

Created by Allex Valentín.

MD-Reviewer works with Markdown documents on your Mac. It does not require an app account and contains no advertising, tracking or analytics service. Document text is not sent to the developer.

Documents, settings and recovery

Files you choose remain in their chosen locations. Reading and page preferences, and private recovery copies, are stored locally. Recovery copies may remain after restoration until you explicitly discard them using File → Manage Recovery Copies. Deleting the original file does not automatically delete a retained recovery copy.

Links and sharing

Opening a web link uses your browser; the destination website has its own privacy practices. Sharing uses the service you choose. Formatted sharing omits the local file-path footer. Print and PDF export may include a file path if you enable that option.

Apple services

Apple processes App Store purchases and may provide system diagnostics under your macOS settings. MD-Reviewer does not include its own telemetry service. Files stored in a cloud-synced folder follow the privacy settings of that provider.

Support

If you choose to send a support request, share only the information needed to describe the problem. Use a disposable example instead of confidential documents. Support requests use public GitHub issues. Anything you post there may be visible to others and is handled under GitHub’s privacy practices. Do not post confidential documents or personal data. See the support page for the issue tracker and private vulnerability-reporting link.
